Questions

Is it really unlimited?

Yes - there's no cap on how many task requests you send. Requests are handled in order, one active request per agent at a time, so bigger plans run more in parallel.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You describe the work in plain words; we build, connect and maintain the agent.

What about my data?

Agents only get the access a task needs, through the official connections of the tools you already use. We never ask for your passwords.

How do discount codes work?

A code takes a percentage off your plan's monthly price for the first 6 months of your subscription. From month 7 you pay the normal price shown above. It is confirmed on your first invoice. Cancelling is still possible any time.

Can I cancel?

Any time, effective at the end of your billing month. No contracts.
